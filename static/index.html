    The two Qwen3-ASR models download from Hugging Face on first use. The quantized variants are made here on this Mac from those same weights, a few minutes once, and stay until you remove them.

    Accepts audio and video files. Up to 4 GB per file: the upload is staged on disk before transcribing, so one job briefly needs twice the file's size free. For a longer video, transcribe its extracted audio track instead.
